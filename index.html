Sources and historical context

This is a reference to the script’s documented modern charts. Claims of prehistoric use are not established here. Woshite is associated with the Hotsuma Tsutae, Mikasafumi, and Futomani texts.

Practitioner’s account of the syllabary describes variant readings, including WU/N. Interpretive claims are distinct from verified historical evidence.

Historical context and interpretation

Katakamuna’s reported discovery is associated with Narasaki Satsuki in 1949. Claims of a prehistoric civilization or scientific powers are part of the tradition’s interpretation, not established conclusions of this research.
